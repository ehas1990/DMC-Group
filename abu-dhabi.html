Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi is swiftly rising as a prominent player in the logistics sector. As the capital of the United Arab Emirates, it benefits from a strategically advantageous geographic location and ambitious infrastructure development initiatives. The Abu Dhabi Port’s Free Zone and Khalifa Port represent pivotal components of the emirate's strategy for logistics growth, providing world-class facilities to enterprises seeking regional expansion. Furthermore, Abu Dhabi's commitment to economic diversification, coupled with substantial investments in transportation infrastructure, is drawing the attention of global logistics giants, positioning the emirate as a promising hub for future logistics sector expansion.

The flourishing e-commerce market and the UAE's unwavering dedication to innovation further amplify the sector's potential. In recognition of these promising prospects, we took the strategic step of establishing our logistics company ‘DMC Global Logistics’ in Abu Dhabi on 2018. This strategic move aligns us with the evolving opportunities in the region, allowing us to contribute to the local logistics ecosystem and serve as a vital partner in facilitating trade.
